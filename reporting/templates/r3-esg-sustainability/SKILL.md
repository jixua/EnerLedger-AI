# ESG and sustainability report

Read `definition.json` before analysis. Do not infer full framework compliance from a partial disclosure.

## Domain decisions

- Organize climate content around governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics/targets when those pillars are selected.
- Separate board oversight from management responsibility and distinguish policies from demonstrated operation.
- Separate physical risks, transition risks, and opportunities; retain time horizon and scenario assumptions when available.
- Reuse organization emissions only with matching reporting period, organizational boundary, unit, and methodology.
- Present targets with base year, target year, scope coverage, metric, and status. Do not turn an aspiration into an approved target.
- Label assurance level and scope precisely; absence of assurance is not proof that disclosed values are false.

Ask for the selected reporting frameworks and reporting year when missing. State which selected framework elements cannot be assessed from available evidence.
