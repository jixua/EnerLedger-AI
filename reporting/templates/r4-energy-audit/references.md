# R4 references

Use `报告生成规范.html` for the initial field inventory. ASHRAE, ISO 50002, GB/T 17166, and domestic diagnostic guides may define different scopes and evidence expectations; select and version them explicitly.

The ASHRAE sample forms, US DOE materials, BCA briefing, and MIIT guides are references with different purposes. A data collection form is not a completed audit report.
